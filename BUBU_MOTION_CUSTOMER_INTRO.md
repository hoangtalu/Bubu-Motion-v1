# Meet Bubu Motion

Bubu Motion is a small AI companion device with a round touch screen, animated eyes, voice interaction, and personality.

It is designed to feel alive: it listens, reacts, remembers your progress, and gives simple daily care interactions like a modern Tamagotchi-style buddy.

## What Bubu Motion Can Do Today

- Connect to Wi-Fi and reconnect automatically if the network drops.
- Receive OTA firmware updates.
- Run stable voice interaction flow: wake word, voice activity detection, and cloud speech/AI/speech response pipeline.
- Save important settings and progress so they stay after reboot.
- Run a full care system with 4 wellbeing stats: Hunger, Mood, Energy, and Cleanliness.
- Let you care for Bubu through actions like Feed, Bath, and Sleep.
- Track XP and level progression when you interact and care for Bubu.
- Show expressive animated eyes with blinking, mood changes, touch reaction, idle movement, and color transitions.
- Provide a touch menu with core navigation, care actions, stats panel, and level panel.

## What This Means For You

Bubu Motion is ready as an interactive companion that can:

- Talk with you by voice.
- React visually with expressive eyes.
- Let you care for it and see progress over time.
- Keep your progress and settings between restarts.
- Stay up to date with firmware updates.

## Coming Next (Planned)

The tracker also lists next features under active build planning, including:

- Reminder system (voice-created reminders with confirmation logic).
- AI tools for emotion control, on-screen messages, and context-aware responses.
- Behavior scheduler (Bubu can initiate context-based conversations).
- Notes memory and speaker identity features.
- Reminder management menu.

These are planned items and not all are shipping in the current V1 release.

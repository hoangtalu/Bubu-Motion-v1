### Wake word request: "Hey Bubu" — English / ESP32-S3

Hi Espressif team,

We would like to request an English wake word for our ongoing project.

- **Wake phrase:** `Hey Bubu`
- **Language:** English
- **Pronunciation:** "hay BOO-boo" — `/heɪ ˈbuːbuː/`. Both syllables of "Bubu"
  take /uː/ as in *boot*, roughly equal length, light stress on the first. It is
  **not** "BUH-buh".
- **Syllables:** 3 — Hey · Bu · bu
- **Target chip:** ESP32-S3
- **Preferred model:** WakeNet10

"Bubu" is a coined name, so we have spelled the pronunciation out rather than
leave it to the spelling.

Bubu (https://bubumotion.vn) is an AI companion toy for children that we are
preparing to ship in Vietnam. Most of the people saying the wake word will be
Vietnamese children, so if the pipeline can include Vietnamese-accented English
voices, that would suit our users well.

Thank you!
